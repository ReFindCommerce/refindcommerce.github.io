- Put your passport, boarding pass, visa or entry documents, travel insurance details and booking confirmations in the order you will use them.

- Keep secure back-ups where useful, and do a quick check before each stage: check-in, security, boarding and arrival.

- A simple system saves time, cuts down rummaging, and helps you move through the airport with less stress.

- The simple rule: one place, one order, one check The easiest way to organise airport travel documents is to follow three rules.

- One place Keep the documents you need at the airport in one travel wallet, document pouch or dedicated bag pocket.
