- What Happens When a Tracker Card Is Out of Range?

- When a tracker card is out of range, your phone usually cannot connect to it directly.

- The app will normally show the last seen location - the last place the card was detected.

- If your card works with Apple Find My or Samsung SmartThings Find, its location may update when a compatible nearby device helps detect it through that network.

- It is useful for finding misplaced items, but it is not continuous live tracking.

- That simple point matters if you are thinking about adding a tracker card to a wallet, purse, card holder, bag, or travel document holder.

Small tracking accessories are most useful when the setup is simple, compatible with the customer device, and easy to keep with the item being tracked.
