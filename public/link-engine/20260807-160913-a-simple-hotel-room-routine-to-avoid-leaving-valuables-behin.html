- Leaving things behind in a hotel is easy when checkout is rushed, you are tired, or everyone is packing at once.

- Start with passports, wallets, phones, bank cards, keys, medication, jewellery, work passes and chargers.

- Keep them together while you pack, not spread across the bed, safe, bathroom and plug sockets.

- Those are the places small but important items often hide.

- Step 1 - Put all valuables in one place before you pack The aim is simple: before anything goes into your bag, all valuables should be visible in one place.

- Avoid putting valuables on the bed if the bedding is still being moved.
