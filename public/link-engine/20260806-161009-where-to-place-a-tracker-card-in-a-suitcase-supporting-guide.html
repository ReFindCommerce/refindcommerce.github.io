- Where to Place a Tracker Card in a Suitcase Put your tracker card somewhere secure, easy to remember, and unlikely to fall out.

- For most suitcases, the best places are an inside zipped pocket, a document pocket, or a secure luggage tag holder attached firmly to the outside of the bag.

- The right choice depends on whether you want the card hidden inside the case, easy to check before you leave home, or kept close to important travel documents.

- Best Places to Put a Tracker Card in a Suitcase The best place is one you will use every trip.

- It should be secure, simple to check, and not in the way when you unpack.

- Inside a zipped pocket An inside zipped pocket is usually the simplest place to start.

Small tracking accessories are most useful when the setup is simple, compatible with the customer device, and easy to keep with the item being tracked.
